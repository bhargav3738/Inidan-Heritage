# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences served evenly by the same page: prospective diners researching the restaurant before visiting in person (menu, story, hours, location), and online orderers who go through the cart and account flow to place a pickup/delivery order. No single job dominates — the surface must work for both browsing-to-visit and browsing-to-order.

## Product Purpose

A single-page restaurant site ("Bombay Heritage" placeholder branding) that presents the menu, chef's specials, and story, and lets a visitor add items to a cart and check out. Success is a visitor either deciding to visit in person or completing an order.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Front-end only: cart, login/signup, and checkout are UI/interaction only. No real backend, payment processor, accounts system, or order fulfillment exists yet — undecided whether one will be added later or how it will be wired in.
- Single static HTML file (Tailwind via CDN, GSAP/ScrollTrigger for scroll reveals, Iconify for icons, Google Fonts Fraunces + Inter).

## Evidence on Hand

- All current content is placeholder: restaurant name ("Bombay Heritage"), address ("42 Spice Lane"), phone, email, founding year, and stats (37+ years, 62 recipes, 4.9★ rating) are sample copy, not a real business's facts.
- No real brand assets exist (logo, original photography, reviews, press). Hero/about imagery are stock photos from Unsplash. Future work must not present placeholder facts or stock imagery as real proof.

## Product Principles

- Keep the marketing (menu/story/specials) and transactional (cart/login/checkout) paths equally strong — neither is secondary.
- Treat all current names, numbers, and copy as swappable filler, not commitments to preserve.
- Don't fabricate a backend or claim real order processing until one actually exists; keep checkout framed as front-end-only until stated otherwise.
